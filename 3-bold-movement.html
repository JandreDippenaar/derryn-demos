What is biokinetics?

Exercise is the treatment

A biokineticist is a registered health professional who uses exercise to rehab injuries, manage conditions and build performance. Here’s how it works.

Assess

Movement, strength and history. We find what’s holding you back.

Train

A programme for the studio and home, progressed every few weeks.

Return

Back to sport, work or daily life, with a plan to stay there.

FAQ

What does a biokineticist do?

Uses exercise as treatment: rehab, return to sport, chronic conditions and getting stronger.

Do I need a referral?

No, just book. Some medical aids want one, so check yours.

Does medical aid cover it?

Most schemes do, from day-to-day or savings. Check your plan.
