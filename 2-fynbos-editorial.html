What is biokinetics?

Exercise, prescribed like medicine

Biokineticists are registered health professionals. We assess how you move, then build an exercise programme to treat an injury or condition and keep it from coming back.

Recover

After injury or surgery, once the acute phase is over.

Manage

Chronic conditions like diabetes, blood pressure and arthritis.

Prevent

Strength, balance and conditioning so you stay well.
