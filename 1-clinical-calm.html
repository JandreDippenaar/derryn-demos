About me

I’ve been a biokineticist for 26 years. I use exercise to help people recover from injury and surgery, and to manage things like diabetes and arthritis.

BSc Sports Science and Honours in Biokinetics, Stellenbosch University. Contract lecturer at UWC since 2007.
