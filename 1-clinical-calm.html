What is biokinetics?

Exercise, prescribed as treatment.

A biokineticist is a registered health professional who uses exercise to treat injuries and manage health conditions. Your programme is built around your body, your history and your goals.

RehabAfter injury or surgery, when physio ends.
Chronic conditionsDiabetes, blood pressure, arthritis.
PreventionStrength and balance to avoid injury.
PerformanceTesting and conditioning for sport.
